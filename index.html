Our Services

Everything your car needs, by hand

From a quick exterior hand wash to a full valet and interior deep clean — every service is carried out by hand with the right care, products and equipment.

Exterior Hand Wash

A full hand wash of the bodywork using soft wash mitts and a proper two-bucket method, then a clean towel dry to a streak-free finish.

  • Bodywork
  • Wheels
  • Hand dry

Mini Valet

Our most popular quick refresh — exterior hand wash plus a vacuum and wipe-down inside, so the car looks and smells clean again.

  • Wash & dry
  • Interior vacuum
  • Windows

Full Valet

A complete inside-and-out valet: hand wash and dry outside, full vacuum, dashboard and trim dressing, interior glass and boot cleaned.

  • Inside & out
  • Trim dressing
  • Boot & mats

Interior Deep Clean

Seats, carpets, mats, headlining and hard surfaces cleaned and freshened — ideal before a sale, after a long winter, or for family cars.

  • Seats & carpets
  • Odour freshen
  • Plastics

Tyres & Alloys

Wheels deep-cleaned of brake dust and road grime, then tyres dressed for that rich satin black finish that lifts the whole car.

  • Alloy clean
  • Brake dust
  • Tyre shine

Polish & Wax

Hand-applied polish and wax to add gloss, depth and protection to your paint — helping water bead and keeping the shine for longer.

  • Gloss boost
  • Paint protection
  • Water beading

About Us

A proper hand car wash, built on repeat customers

R HAND CAR WASH is a hand car wash and valeting business on Moston Lane in Manchester (M9 4HU). We're a hard-working, friendly local team who do things the traditional way: everything is washed, dried and finished by hand, never through an automatic brush machine that can dull your paint and leave swirl marks.

That approach has earned us a 4.7-star rating from 408 Google reviews — and, more importantly, a steady stream of neighbours who come back week after week. Customers tell us it's the friendly welcome, the modern equipment and the sensible prices that keep them coming back.

Whether you need a quick exterior wash on the way home, a full valet before a big trip, or an interior deep clean after a long winter, we'll fit it in and treat your car like it's our own.
